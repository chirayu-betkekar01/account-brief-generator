# Cost Optimization pillar of the Well-Architected Framework

Plain-English summary of the linked AWS page, written for this demo (paraphrased, not a quotation). Check the source for current detail.

Key points

- The aim is a workload that fully uses its resources and achieves its outcome at the lowest price point that still meets functional requirements.
- The guidance is organised around five goals: practise cloud financial management, build awareness of expenditure and usage, use cost-effective resources, manage demand and supply of resources, and keep optimising over time.
- On premises, cost optimisation is hard because capacity must be predicted in advance and procurement is slow. In the cloud, capacity can be matched to demand.
- The paper is written for finance as well as technology roles, including CFOs, financial planners and analysts alongside architects and developers. It gives principles and references, not implementation detail.

Useful when: opening the finance conversation: cost is a shared discipline across teams, not a one-off purchase decision.
