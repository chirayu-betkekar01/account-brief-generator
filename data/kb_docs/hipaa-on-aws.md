# Using AWS for HIPAA workloads

Plain-English summary of the linked AWS page, written for this demo (paraphrased, not a quotation). Check the source for current detail.

Key points

- AWS acts as a business associate under HIPAA and offers a standard Business Associate Addendum (BAA), which customers accept through AWS Artifact.
- Protected health information should only be processed with services on the HIPAA Eligible Services list, which AWS extends over time.
- The customer must protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of the PHI they create, transmit, receive or maintain, with suitable administrative, physical and technical controls. The shared responsibility model applies.
- PHI includes insurance and billing information, diagnosis data, clinical care data and lab results.

Useful when: healthcare customers moving patient systems or imaging archives to AWS.

Source: https://aws.amazon.com/compliance/hipaa-compliance/ (checked 2026-10-07)
